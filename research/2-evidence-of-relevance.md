2.1 Student questionnaire and sample

The initial evidence comes from the Google Forms questionnaire “AITU University Events: Student Experience”. The snapshot contains five response records submitted on October 22–23, 2026, in Asia/Almaty time. All five selected year 3. Reported attendance over the last three months was 1–2 events in two records, 3–5 in two records, and “I do not remember” in one. The analysis uses rows 2–6 of the linked response sheet; later responses require a new snapshot.

2.3 Evidence limits and follow-up discussions

The small, self-selected sample contains only third-year responses. It supplies no verified organizer workload, baseline timings, client agreement, or measured platform outcomes. Two records selected “No desire to participate”, one alongside specific barriers. Such answers should not be treated as problems that software alone can solve.
