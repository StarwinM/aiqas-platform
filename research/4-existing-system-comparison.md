4 Existing system comparison

Four systems were selected: CampusGroups, Anthology Engage, Modern Campus Involve (formerly Presence), and Eventbrite. The first three are oriented toward university activity; Eventbrite was chosen as an event platform to compare registration and attendance. The matrix is based on official pages [A1–A8] as of October 11, 2026, without access to paid demo environments.

The designation "not established" means the capability was not confirmed by the chosen sources; it does not mean the absence of the feature. No separate technical testing of the systems was conducted. AIQAS is described here as a project, not as a ready competitor.

Conclusion: registration, attendance tracking, and university activity history are already presented in analogs. Therefore, the rationale for AIQAS should rely on a confirmed local process, agreed languages, access to integrations, data rules, and pilot capability. In-house development will allow exploring the chosen cycle and engineering solutions, but its superiority over commercial products is not yet proven. Cost, localization, and hosting require separate clarification; they are not declared as shortcomings of analogs without confirmation.
